water_drop

Hydration

Ensure you drink at least 8-10 glasses of water daily to help flush toxins from your system.

child_care

Breastfeeding

Frequent feeding helps both mother and baby clear bilirubin and maintain energy levels.

bedtime

Rest & Recovery

Prioritize sleep. Your liver recovers best when your body is well-rested and not under stress.

medical_services

Medical Consultation

If symptoms persist or jaundice deepens, contact your doctor immediately for blood tests.
